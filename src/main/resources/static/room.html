Instructions

Everything you need to know to get started

Paint Brush

Allows you to draw on the canvas

Bucket

To use the bucket the Paint Brush must be selected. This allows you to draw a stroke which auto encloses, and once enclosed it will be filled with the bucket color that you have selected.

Pan & Zoom

Allows you to pan the canvas and zoom in on the canvas. To pan click on the canvas and move your mouse, to zoom use your scroll wheel.

Brush & Bucket Color

The first color represents your brush color. The second color represents your bucket color.

Paint Thickness

Allows you to select the thickness of your paint brush

Homepage

Makes you leave the room and returns you to the homepage

Save

Downloads the current canvas into a canvas.png image

Color Palette

All the colors palettes you can choose from for your Paint Brush color or your Paint Bucket color.

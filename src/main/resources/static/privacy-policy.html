This Privacy Policy explains how Paint Station collects, uses, stores, and protects information when you use Paint Station.io(the "Service").

By using the Service, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information We Collect

The Service allows users to create and join temporary collaborative drawing rooms without creating an account.

Information Provided When Using a Room

  • A display name or username you choose to use in the room.
  • The name of the room.
  • A room password, if the room is password-protected.
  • Drawing and canvas data created by participants in the room.
  • Information necessary to maintain and synchronize the room between participants.

We do not require users to provide an email address, telephone number, real name, or other account information to use the Service.

Room Passwords

Room passwords are temporary credentials used to control access to password-protected rooms.

We do not store room passwords in readable plaintext. Room passwords are stored using a one-waypassword hashing mechanism so that the original password cannot be recovered from the stored value.

We do not record passwords or submitted password attempts in our application logs.

Users should not use a room password that they use for another account or service.

2. Temporary Room Data

The Service is designed to use temporary rooms.

While a room is active, we may temporarily store or process:

  • The room name.
  • Usernames or display names of participants.
  • Information identifying participants currently in the room.
  • Drawing and canvas data.
  • The hashed password associated with a password-protected room.
  • Other temporary information necessary to operate the room.

This information is used to provide the collaborative drawing and room functionality.

When a room is closed, normally after all participants have left, the room and its associated temporary room data are deleted from the application's active storage.

This means that room-related information may no longer be available for access or deletion requests after the room has been deleted.

Deletion from active application storage does not necessarily mean immediate deletion from every technical backup or infrastructure system. Where applicable, information may remain temporarily in backups or other infrastructure systems until those systems automatically overwrite or delete it.

3. Chat

The Service provides real-time chat between participants in a room.

Chat messages are transmitted through the Service to participants in the relevant room. We do notintentionally store chat messages in a permanent database.

Chat data may temporarily exist in server memory or other transient systems as necessary to provide the real-time communication functionality.

Other participants may be able to copy, screenshot, or otherwise preserve messages that they receive.

Users should not share sensitive, confidential, or personally identifying information through the chat feature.

4. Logs and Activity Information

We maintain technical, operational, and security logs that may record events such as room creation, room deletion, successful room joins, failed room access attempts, and other security-related events. These logs may include information such as the event type and the date and time at which the event occurred.

Depending on the event, these logs may record information such as:

  • Room creation events.
  • Room closure events.
  • Successful room joins.
  • Failed attempts to join password-protected rooms.
  • Room access events.
  • Security-related events.
  • Application errors and technical events.
  • Timestamps associated with these events.
  • Other information reasonably necessary to operate, troubleshoot, secure, or prevent abuse of theService.

We do not intentionally record room passwords or submitted password attempts in these logs.

These logs are maintained separately from the temporary room data and may remain after the associated room has been deleted.

We retain logs for 90 days unless a longer retention period is reasonably necessaryfor security, legal, fraud-prevention, dispute-resolution, or other legitimate operational purposes.

After the applicable retention period, logs are deleted or overwritten according to our retention practices.

5. Information We Do Not Intentionally Collect

The application does not require or intentionally collect:

  • User accounts.
  • Email addresses.
  • Telephone numbers.
  • Real names.
  • Permanent user profiles.
  • Precise location information.
  • IP addresses through the application's own user-data systems.
  • Permanent copies of drawings.
  • Permanent copies of chat messages.

However, the operating system, hosting provider, or other infrastructure used to operate the Service may generate technical logs as part of normal operation.

6. How We Use Information

We use information processed through the Service to:

  • Create and operate collaborative drawing rooms.
  • Allow users to join rooms.
  • Authenticate access to password-protected rooms.
  • Synchronize drawings between participants.
  • Provide real-time communication.
  • Maintain room functionality.
  • Prevent unauthorized access and abuse.
  • Detect and investigate security incidents.
  • Diagnose technical problems.
  • Maintain and improve the reliability and security of the Service.

We do not sell personal information.

We do not use room data to create advertising profiles.

7. User-Generated Content

Users may create drawings, choose display names, and communicate with other participants.

Room content is temporary, but users should understand that other participants can view content while participating in a room and may independently copy, screenshot, record, or otherwise preserve it.

We cannot control what another participant does with information or content that they can access.

Users should not include sensitive personal information in drawings, usernames, room names, or chat messages.

Additional rules concerning user-generated content are described in our Terms of Service and Community Guidelines.

Information processed by the Service may therefore be stored or processed on AWS infrastructure as necessary to provide the Service.

We may also use infrastructure and services provided by AWS to operate, monitor, secure, and maintain the Service.

We do not currently use third-party advertising or analytics services.

If we begin using additional third-party services that process personal information, we will update this Privacy Policy as appropriate.

9. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

The Service does not intentionally use advertising cookies or third-party tracking technologies for advertising purposes.

The Service may use technically necessary browser storage, cookies, session identifiers, or similar technologies when necessary to operate the Service.

We do not use these technologies to create advertising profiles.

10. Data Security

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information processed through the Service against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction.

These measures may include:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption.
  • Password hashing for password-protected rooms.
  • Restrictions on access to server infrastructure.
  • Avoiding storage of passwords in application logs.
  • Deleting temporary room data when rooms are closed.
  • Monitoring security-related events.
  • Maintaining security and technical logs for a limited period.

No method of transmitting or storing information over the Internet is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

11. Data Retention

Different types of information have different retention periods.

Temporary Room Data

Room names, usernames/display names, participant information, drawing/canvas data, and room password hashes are intended to exist only while their associated room is active.

When the room closes, this information is deleted from the application's active storage.

Chat Data

Chat messages are not intentionally stored permanently and are intended to exist only as necessary to transmit them to current participants.

Logs

Technical, operational, and security logs are retained separately from room data for 90 days.

Logs may therefore remain after a room and its associated room data have been deleted.

We may retain certain information for a longer period where reasonably necessary to comply with legal obligations, protect the Service, investigate security incidents, prevent fraud or abuse, resolve disputes, orprotect our legal rights.

Paint Station does not require users to provide their age, real name, email address, or account information. Because users can use the Service without an account or persistent identity, we generally cannot determine the age or identity of an anonymous user.

If we become aware that a particular user is under 13 and that applicable law requires us to take action, we will take reasonable steps appropriate to the circumstances.
